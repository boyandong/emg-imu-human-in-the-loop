# DS2 public-v8 unmatched block: long-group diagnostic

The [exact waveform join](DS2_TDMS_RAW_EXACT_JOIN_AUDIT.json) leaves 30 consecutive raw-MAT trials, zero-based indices 389–418, without a TDMS group or subject identity. All 30 have public gesture code 0. They occur between the last verified subject-folder-3 join at index 388 and the next verified subject-folder-4 join at index 419. This positional context suggests looking at `SEMG-04/SEMG-04_Mv1.tdms`, which contains one long group of 980,100 samples per channel, **but does not establish a match**.

The [diagnostic script](scripts/audit_ds2_unmatched_long_group.py) checks the archived ZIP, MAT and existing join hashes. It searches every full-length offset in that long group's first channel for the strongest Pearson correlation with each of the 30 MAT trials. A positive control, the independently exact-matched MAT trial 388 against its TDMS group, returns **r ≈ 1.0 at offset zero**. For the unmatched 30, the largest absolute first-channel correlation is only **0.1321**. Selected first/middle/last trials were also compared across all nine raw-to-TDMS channel pairs; their largest absolute correlation is **0.1773**. The full offsets and correlation matrix are saved in [the audit JSON](DS2_UNMATCHED_LONG_GROUP_AUDIT.json).

This rules out a straightforward time-shifted linear copy in the tested positional candidate; it does **not** rule out nonlinear preprocessing, an entirely different source, or another archival error. Neither this check nor the MAT ordering supplies a reliable subject or low/medium/high force label. The 30 trials remain excluded from subject-held-out results, and DS2 force-stratified experiments remain blocked pending an authoritative mapping.

The parser dependency is the `tdms` extra in `emgimu_classifier/pyproject.toml` (`nptdms==1.11.0`); this workstation also has it in `../work/python_vendor`. Reproduce from the repository root with `PYTHONPATH=../work/python_vendor` and `D:/miniconda/python.exe emgimu_classifier/benchmarks/discovery/scripts/audit_ds2_unmatched_long_group.py --archive ../work/datasets/historical_ds2_candidate/ds2_kaggle_v8.zip --raw-mat ../work/datasets/historical_ds2_candidate/Data_all_Raw.mat --join-csv emgimu_classifier/benchmarks/discovery/DS2_TDMS_RAW_EXACT_JOIN.csv --join-audit emgimu_classifier/benchmarks/discovery/DS2_TDMS_RAW_EXACT_JOIN_AUDIT.json --mat-join-csv emgimu_classifier/benchmarks/discovery/DS2_MAT_TRIAL_WINDOW_JOIN.csv --output emgimu_classifier/benchmarks/discovery/DS2_UNMATCHED_LONG_GROUP_AUDIT.json`.
