# S04 cue-relative continuous decoder events

The [F0 protocol](CUE_EVENT_PROTOCOL.json) and [runner](../song_cue_event_audit.py) were committed as `1f7a57c` before computing its result. The [current F0+SPD protocol](SPD_CUE_EVENT_PROTOCOL.json) and common runner were frozen as `2c4b0bd` before its result. Both replay the complete saved S04 eight-channel raw stream through the corresponding causal Song model and the fixed 0.5 probability/three-frame `SongOnlineDecision` rule. The raw HDF5 and model hashes match their protocols. This extends the earlier [frame-level continuous replay](CONTINUOUS_REPLAY_AUDIT.json) with one row for each of 144 valid completed formal cue events. Per-event rows, unrounded aggregates and native-annotation read-back checks exist for [F0](CUE_EVENT_AUDIT.json) and [F0+SPD](SPD_CUE_EVENT_AUDIT.json).

Only model windows wholly inside the recorded stable cue interval count. An event succeeds if the decoded state matches its cue at least once; the endpoint check asks whether the final such window matches. The first-correct time is measured from the **recorded stable cue start** to the end of the first matching decoded model window. It includes up to 200 ms of window formation plus the three-frame persistence rule and is not a measured physiological onset or display latency.

Across the 108 active cues, F0 matches at least once in 92 and ends correctly in 91; current F0+SPD matches and ends correctly in 100. The [paired audit](CUE_EVENT_COMPARISON.json) finds nine F0 misses recovered by F0+SPD, all Index Pinch, and one newly missed Fist. Eight current-model active cues still never reach the correct state: six Index Pinch and two Fist. Among successful active cues, the current model's pooled first-correct median is 0.288 s and 90th percentile is recorded in the [unrounded audit](SPD_CUE_EVENT_AUDIT.json). In the final 400 ms of the pre-prompt rest interval, the decoded state is active for at least one frame in 16/144 F0 events versus 28/144 current-model events; 17 new cases and five resolved cases produce that net increase. These may reflect prior-movement carryover rather than new spurious onsets.

The offline event result explains why high **whole-trial averaged** accuracy can coexist with poor moment-to-moment use, especially for pinch. The current model improves recorded pinch detection but also spends more late-rest cue intervals in an active state. The saved S04 cues still do not measure actual movement onset/offset, actual screen updates or USB latency. S04 is the same participant and day used in prior exploration, so these are diagnostic counts, not a new independent product-acceptance test. Open-hand success here also does not contradict the user's live report because the physical electrode placement, calibration and in-use signal may differ. New time-stamped live annotations are still needed for that question.
